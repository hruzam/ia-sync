For an audit, `AGENTS.md` is the existing entry map; unchanged-meaning pointer
repairs there are the local mechanics-diff target. Changing its rules, read order,
roles or authority is a substantive finding, not a mechanics repair. No keeper
transfer or new project wrapper is implied. If no mechanics repair is supported,
report `none` and leave the proposed diff empty. Output filenames and the bed are assigned
before an audit writes; this layer does not open a session or take another head's pen.
