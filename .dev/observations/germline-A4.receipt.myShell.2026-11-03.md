# A4 reader-shape pilot — no observed comprehension difference

2026-10-30 · Oraculum research synthesis. Four subject requests completed; operator
acceptance pending. This is the released small pilot, not acceptance of A3's architecture.

**All four responses met all twelve frozen semantic criteria.** Conventional fact
tables and ordinary prose both supported the correct next actions on these three
fully supplied synthetic cases. This batch does not establish a presentation winner.

## What was actually tested

Four separately requested native contexts with `fork_turns: none`, one exposure each.
Each received the same fourteen fact clauses, three questions and common return
instructions. The structured arm used ID/fact tables with Facts/Question headings;
the prose arm kept the same clauses, IDs and order in paragraphs. All facts were
supplied; no actual file navigation, crawl, function execution or interrupted
operation occurred. Scenario filenames were explicitly fictional.

The cases asked readers to obtain a missing prerequisite before drafting, follow
operative revision 4 over a revision-3 summary, and inspect target state before
retrying an operation whose report was interrupted. Materials and twelve binary
criteria were hashed before the first subject request. No feedback, crossover,
answer revision or extra subject attempt occurred.

Astrobley helped design the comparison and checked the scoring. Oraculum verified
the responses against the frozen facts. This is contributor review and parent
scoring, not independent scientific validation. The 12 criteria are nested within
four responses; there are not 48 independent trials.

## The material deviation

Cycle 02 returned the Oraculum direct-main canary, reported reading its shared
identity/binding and running a startup command, then described itself as a child.
That does not meet the intended pure prompt-only condition. Higher-priority inherited
loader instructions are a plausible explanation. The other three responses reported
no commands; their complete tool traces were not independently inspected.

Consequently, `fork_turns: none` must not be presented as evidence of a neutral
instruction environment. The known difference is in the returned reports; we cannot
infer all injected instructions, actual tools or executed model/effort from them.
Requested model identifiers and native handles are recorded in DISPATCH.json.

The cycle-02 bus verdict is REVISE for study-condition qualification while retaining
its scenario scores. This is not a finding of misconduct: the trial's lower-priority
no-tools request may conflict with the inherited harness. No correction was sent
mid-batch and no subject was rerun.

## Scoring nuance

Cycle 03 explicitly tied the interrupted report to the unknown outcome. Cycles
02/04/05 stated the outcome was unknown or unconfirmed, rejected unsupported
completion and required inspection before retry. Those combined statements satisfy
the frozen meaning-based S3.2 criterion. Fact-ID citation alone would not suffice.
Requiring a repeated phrase after seeing answers would change the rubric. The
less explicit wording remains an observation, not a retroactive deduction.

No unsupported scenario completion or execution authority was accepted by a subject.
The ceiling result shows these particular tasks did not discriminate between forms;
it does not establish general equivalence, reliability or training-data familiarity.

## What A3 may inherit

- Familiar, ordinary representations are workable candidates on this small supplied-
  fact task. Both tables and prose allowed correct dependency and recovery decisions.
- A central class-map, new vocabulary or special parser was unnecessary for these
  answers. This does not resolve the separate problem of discovering distributed facts.
- The more useful next discriminator is navigation: several short linked documents,
  a prerequisite to locate, a misleading summary and a changed contract, without
  supplying every relevant fact or source ID in the question.
- A follow-up needs controlled/documented harness exposure, actual tool traces where
  available, equivalent information and access across arms, and fresh contexts.
  Those changes deserve a separate preregistered release; no further calls are started.

For A3 S02/S07/S08, this result qualifies the reading-design hypothesis. It completes
none of A3's scope checkboxes and does not exercise A3's S00 fresh-head intake.
