## The consumer promise

Give the reader enough understanding to take the assigned next step, plus exact
routes to recover what becomes relevant. Every load-bearing inherited claim must
make these three answers obtainable:

1. **May inherit:** what it says, its status and why it applies to this task.
2. **Must verify:** the source/revision, authority or observed effect this action depends on.
3. **Would invalidate:** a superseding decision, changed dependency, contradictory
   observation or other stated condition that requires reconsideration.

These are required design semantics selected by Majkee in this conversation. Their
representation remains a candidate. Do not repeat them mechanically on every paragraph.
Unknown validity is a visible gap; a plausible summary does not fill it.

## Five knowledge roles, with natural homes

These are logical bins. Existing files keep their homes; five new folders are unnecessary.

| Role | Owns | Typical expansion trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Operative sources and contracts | Current obligations, interfaces, effect boundaries | Before depending on a rule or performing its operation |
| Decisions and their reasons | Acceptance, rejected alternatives, tradeoffs and supersession | A design assumption or choice is implicated |
| Observations and receipts | What happened, with method/revision and limits | Completion, behavior or recovery is uncertain |
| Open questions and anomalies | Unresolved evidence, competing explanations, next discriminator | A contradiction or unexpected tail affects the task |
| History retained for explanation | Earlier frames and why the present shape arose | Current reasons are insufficient or disputed |

A useful record answers who observed/decided what, where its evidence lives, why it
matters and what it supersedes. A finding may touch several roles; retain provenance
without duplicating normative wording or forcing an anomaly into a tidy category.
