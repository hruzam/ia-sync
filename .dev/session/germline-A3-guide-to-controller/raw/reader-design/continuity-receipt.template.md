# Continuity receipt — candidate template

This is an unfilled template, not evidence that a handoff succeeded. Owner: the
receiving head. First A3 use: a section titled `Continuity receipt` inside
`raw/RESULT.S00.md`. The receipt records actual use; STATUS retains the live next action.

Use on a fresh handoff, substantial context recovery or return after a relevant
source change. Do not create one per ordinary turn. A later qualifying re-entry
uses a new `raw/CONTINUITY.<sequence>.md`, with the next unused sequence, and points
to the earlier receipt instead of copying it. This naming is an A3-local candidate.

Keep the return short; expand only where failure, disagreement or a useful lesson
needs evidence. Mark unknowns. Record substantive partial recovery before yielding.

## Arrival

- Reader/seat, date and runtime; model/effort only as observed or explicitly requested.
- Assignment, phase and checkpoint inherited; exact entry source and predecessor receipt.
- Sources actually consulted, with relevant revisions; what was unavailable.

## Usable inheritance

- Consequential decisions/distinctions carried forward, with their evidence and status.
- What was safe to inherit for this action; what required verification; what would
  invalidate the inherited application. Show one concrete consequence for the task.

## Expansion and friction

- Missing question → why it mattered → direct read or helper → source/result.
- Stale claims, contradictions, unnecessary reads, missed tails or operator rescue.
- Inspected versus uninspected scope. Preserve conflicting evidence and uncertainty.

## Cost and limits

- Main intake, helper work, setup, verification, corrections and elapsed time where
  actually measured; identify measurement method. Otherwise write `unknown`.
- Distinguish rough estimates from telemetry and fresh-context trials from ordinary
  continuation. Do not infer total savings from shorter main intake alone.

## Outcome and repair

- `usable`, `partial` or `blocked`, with the evidence supporting that disposition.
- Exact pointer/account/contract repair made, or proposed repair with an owner.
- Remaining uncertainty and its effect on the next action; STATUS owns that action.
- For a failed intake, retain what remained reusable rather than resetting all history.

## Custody

- Where this receipt and unique supporting evidence are retained.
- Which unresolved finding belongs to which scope/owner and what triggers follow-up.
- Before closure/pruning: preserved destination or commit pin, and surviving entry
  route. An intended destination or content hash alone is not preservation proof.

## Reuse decision owed

A3 S10 must use the observed receipt to decide: amend, adopt for a bounded setting,
defer with named owner/trigger, or reject with reason. Candidate durable homes are
the shared RUNBOOK guide's handoff/closure guidance, with STATUS keeping only a
pointer and the relevant skill referring to the one method source. Inspect existing
homes before selecting one; this template changes no shared guide or global policy.
Preserve the proposal and evidence before pruning even if general adoption is deferred.
