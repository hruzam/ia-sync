# The role I held

**I was Majkee’s attachable decision desk.** In `helloWorld:seniorDev`, I was the place he came to hear what needed his decision, ask why, and say what he wanted in his own words. I also looked at the files and the live pane when the next step depended on what was actually there. This was his mouth, ears and eyes role inside a running project. It combined explanation, faithful recording and a small amount of explicitly assigned execution.

| Seat | Authority in the relay sittings |
|---|---|
| Majkee | Gives decisions, releases and gavels. Can authorize bounded practical work directly. |
| Decision desk (`exec`) | Presents one decision at a time, records the exact answer, and returns the result to the lead. Gives its own recommendation when asked and credits it as such. |
| Lead | Holds STATUS and converts decisions into scoped assignments. Coordinates the run and issues its verdicts. |
| Controller and auditor | Execute assigned operations and independently check evidence, respectively. |

## How it worked in practice

- **Turn a request into one decision.** The lead provided a POINT and a short sheet. I acknowledged the poke promptly, described what the choice would change, and asked one question. When Majkee needed a deeper explanation, I read the source and came back to the same question. I kept his exact words beside any summary he confirmed under R1. An early bundled confirmation produced an ambiguous push record and required a separate clarification. Later sheets used one question per confirmation.
- **Act when the operator asks for a specific effect.** On direct instruction I prepared shell-only tmux windows and delegated one custody task to the `astrobley` child. The child owned the script, copy and ledger pointer. I kept the decision record and independently checked the eight copied inputs against the committed hashes. These assignments show how the seat could take practical work within an explicit remit.
- **Check the recipient and the outcome separately.** A window can keep its name while the agent behind it changes. For one reply bell, the current STATUS and live footer confirmed that Flight’s successor had taken the same pane. I checked the composer for a draft or dialog, staged one labelled pointer, verified it, and submitted once. Claude showed the pointer as queued. The written RETURN was the handoff, and queue acceptance confirmed only the bell’s immediate outcome.

## What this offers the supervisor intake

An attachable operator surface is useful when it keeps the operator’s decision, the scope of the next action, and the evidence of what happened legible at the same time. The broader `supervisor` authority is for 006 to define. This experience supports naming the current remit explicitly, including who owns the gate and whether the seat may act, delegate, or only present and return. Open shell access and a stable window are equipment. The relay work exercised this arrangement in one Codex desk with Claude leads on home. It leaves the Claude equivalent of the desk and cross-vendor supervisor authority to be tested.
