## Weakest assumption

**That "substance vs map" cuts along the file boundary (GUIDE vs chapter).** It does not. Each
GUIDE self-declares as *the canonical shape* of its artifact with a mandatory-field anatomy —
status/GUIDE.md L47–48: "These fields are all mandatory; `none` is a value, while silence is
ambiguity." If `delegated:` / `join_when:` (STATUS) and `turn:` / `deferred:` (VERDICT) exist
only in a chapter, then a seat reading the GUIDE — which is exactly what its prompts direct —
sees a complete-looking schema that is silently incomplete. A GUIDE whose anatomy omits legal
fields is not a lean map; it is a **wrong map**, and by writer rule 4 (trust disk over doc,
guide-writing/GUIDE.md L96–98) the guide becomes the bug on day one. The honest cut is:
**schema stubs in the owning GUIDE (field name, optionality, one line, pointer) · doctrine in
the chapter (semantics, laws, loop, failure modes).** Precedent already in canon:
status/GUIDE.md L12–14 points to runbook/GUIDE.md for the authority model instead of restating
it — pointing is legal; omission is not.

Second-order corollary: a single chapter under one slug defining another slug's artifact
fields collides with the one-topic slug law (guide-writing L37–39) and with the countersign's
own relocation principle ("the fix for duplicated authority is itself duplicated — the audit's
own scar", T2.countersign §Canon destination). The chapter carries *coordination doctrine*
(one topic: fan-out under a head — legitimately `chapter-of: runbook`, since runbook/GUIDE.md
L9 owns "session SHAPE"); it does not become the schema-of-record for STATUS or BUS artifacts.

## Is the legacy frontmatter/manifest repair truly mechanical?

**~80% yes, with three seams that are decisions, not conversions:**

1. **Gavel-status metadata has no slot.** The backtick headers carry authority state —
   "both DRAFT, awaiting majkee gavel" vs "GAVELED by majkee 2026-09-03" (runbook/GUIDE.md
   L7–8). The required five-field frontmatter (guide-writing L72–80) cannot express it, and
   the manifest's four classes (L60–64) have no draft/gaveled axis. Where that state lands
   (body Lineage section? manifest annotation?) is a ruling, not a mechanical move.
2. **`verified:` cannot be truthfully populated by a repair pass.** The staleness organ keys
   on it (guide-writing L84). Stamping the touch date fabricates verification; the repair
   must carry the original gavel/verification dates. Small, but it is semantics, not format.
3. **Any edit to a gaveled GUIDE is a canon edit.** reposoma/AGENTS.md L56: canon is gaveled
   by the operator; agent-authored-direct canon is forbidden (0002 · Force 4). A "format-only"
   repair of gaveled text needs either majkee's pre-gavel of a format-only exemption or
   inclusion in the same gavel as the fold. Do not let "mechanical" smuggle it past the gate.

Note in the fold's favor: the legacy state is **already drift by current law** — three GUIDEs
lack required frontmatter, and the runbook slug holds four unlisted res/ files, violating the
orphan rule (guide-writing L66–68) today. The repair is owed regardless of the fan-out fold;
it just is not free of judgment.
