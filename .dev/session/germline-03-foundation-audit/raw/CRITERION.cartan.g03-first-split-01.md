# Permitted later method

Only static inspection of the final frozen manifest is permitted. No runtime loading, execution,
deployment, source modification, recursive scope expansion, or inference from names alone is
allowed.

## Required outcomes

- **split evidenced** — citations inside the frozen manifest establish all three markers: a named
  controller/selection responsibility, a separately named function/procedure responsibility, and
  an explicit relationship by which the former selects, delegates to, or invokes the latter.
- **not evidenced within frozen scope** — every frozen entry has been inspected under the same
  markers and no complete three-marker set is cited. This is not a claim about files outside the
  manifest and does not close any of the seven audit inputs.
- **unresolved** — a needed source is absent or unreadable, a marker or relationship is ambiguous,
  source identity changes, or an additional source/design decision is needed. Stop rather than
  broadening the method.

## Citation and stop rule

Every later disposition must cite absolute path, frozen repository identity and hash, line range,
and the marker(s) observed or absent under the declared method. Stop immediately on a missing
manifest mapping, hash mismatch, ambiguous criterion, scope expansion request, or design/
implementation question. Atlas may challenge this draft before it freezes.
