# Future Germline-04 guide-dogfood substrate

## Purpose and boundary

If separately opened, Germline-04 audits the Sella ↔ germline ↔
program/controller-design seam with source-accounted evidence. Sella is the primary foundation
under examination, not a completed law. The audit may recommend a Sella adjustment, but it does
not adjust Sella, compose a program, make a canonical decision, or implement anything.

This is not a Germline-04 RUNBOOK or STATUS, an opening authorization, a worker launch, or a bus
cycle. G03's F1 mapping edge and A2 finding remain separate G03 matters.

`/home/hruzam/reposoma/raw.guides/sella/GUIDE.md`. The opening gavel must freeze that source's
immutable identity before the turn begins.

turn.

## Future cycle shape

One bounded audit-only worker may be held for two or three deliberate turns only under the
opening/cycle gavels. Every authorized one-source turn receives an owned durable receipt; Cartan
verifies it before a later source is proposed or a later turn begins. The worker and Oraculum
return chat-only analysis and have no raw or bus pen.

Cartan alone may write a G04 `raw/` working synthesis after verifying those returns. If a later
cycle explicitly activates Atlas, Cartan alone owns POINT and VERDICT, Atlas owns RETURN, and the
raw synthesis supplies the bounded POINT method. No `_bus/` exists or is authorized by this
substrate.

## Conditional reconciliation

Fable/Astra is not standing work. It may be separately authorized only when a receipt identifies
a concrete conflict between cited, versioned source claims that materially changes ownership or
interpretation. Its evidence-only return is: the conflicting passages, whether the difference is
scope or contradiction, and the unresolved decision. It does not reread broadly, edit Sella,
compose a program, or decide canon.
