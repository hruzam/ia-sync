# Living session understanding — exploratory brief

Date: 2026-10-28 · Cartan, with Oraculum and Astrobley consultations

Status: discussion substrate requested by Majkee. This is not a G03 finding, an
accepted architecture, a change to its gate or review inputs, or authority to build.
G03's STATUS and its existing review retain their own ownership and boundaries.

## Need

An entering or continuing AI participant should find enough current, attributable
session understanding for its assignment without reconstructing the whole history.
The participant must be able to expand its reading, challenge a summary, and return
new evidence or a correction. A changing procedure needs a home that does not silently
compete with the present doing-state.

## Candidate shapes

- A living readable working understanding: questions, interpretations, evidence
  pointers, decisions and unresolved disagreements, with scope and attribution visible.
- A derived view or index over existing files; source versions and the extraction or
  synthesis rule affect freshness. A changed source may invalidate only some entries.
- A task-conditioned entry packet pointing to the relevant view and source slices.
  This is a recommendation for where to begin, not a limit on investigation.

These can be alternatives or complements. Neither format, a mandatory new file,
nor automatic authority is decided. A shared representation should allow competing
interpretations rather than turn one summary into unquestioned truth. The contextual
assignment and applicable constraints can be interpreted and recorded for correction;
the act of recording does not turn a proposal into an approval.

Distinguish exact mechanical extraction, indexing, version checks and dependency
tracking from agent judgment about relevance and meaning. A generated synthesis is
an attributed claim with sources and limits. A hash detects changed bytes, not truth,
completeness, live execution, or the validity of an authorization.

## Change and execution

Consider separate revisions for the agreement (goal, acceptance, scope and authority),
the procedure, and an assignment's base. A change should identify affected work,
record its decision, inform relevant participants and reconcile earlier returns.
An old return may remain evidence even if its assignment no longer matches the current
need. Copying context into a fork does not itself transfer the head's STATUS ownership.
This is a design hypothesis; current runbook law requires a sibling when a gate changes.

Test a newcomer, a change of decision or procedure, and a fork returning against an
older understanding. Include stale STATUS, partial or changed returns, incorrect
binding, uncertain execution, late results and duplicate dispatch. Measure useful
retrieval, correct next actions, stale-input recognition and actual reading/coordination
cost rather than assuming a shorter text is better.

## Evidence and limits

Existing STATUS, BUS and fan-out provide recovery and attributed exchange; they are
not necessarily a complete replayable event log or a general semantic cache. The
personal session-coordination trial supplies bounded context practices, not a proven
shared cache. The earlier runbook-upgrade arc exposed a 501-line STATUS, recovery
confusion and costly ceremony; its original producer gate was not met.

Astrobley identified materialized views, incremental build dependencies and
task-conditioned retrieval as relevant established patterns. RAW-RAG investigates
retrieval from original conversation history, with offline evaluation limits; STAIR
investigates structure-aware section retrieval. C2C is different: a learned fusion of
model-internal KV caches, not a durable session record or demonstrated authority
transfer. None qualifies a live coordination system here; no experiments were rerun.
