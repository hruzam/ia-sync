## Work for a successor

1. Inspect RUNBOOK and collateral, including STATUS, BUS and runbook chapters, for
   constraints that may over-cage adaptation, inquiry or participation. For each,
   identify its protected failure, observed cost, and a proportionate alternative.
   Distinguish gaveled law, drafts and recommendations; retain protective invariants.
2. Map existing mechanisms and evidence, including this pilot's old failure cases;
   distinguish present capability from a proposed shared knowledge mechanism.
3. Walk one concrete scenario: entrant, changed decision or procedure, and a fork
   returning against earlier understanding. Compare living document, derived view,
   index, and simpler use of existing surfaces; avoid locking a format in advance.
4. Examine contract and procedure changes, contextual authority, ownership, freshness,
   read expansion, contradictory evidence and return reconciliation. Use selected
   cases: stale STATUS, partial/changed RETURN, wrong binding, unknown execution,
   late results and duplicate dispatch.
5. Present Majkee an evidence-backed GO/AMEND/STOP choice. State whether the scenario
   is a worked example or disposable prototype and what it actually establishes.

No production tool, mandatory packet, fixed model roster, assumed identity-profile
prerequisite, deployment or canon change is part of this proposal. The identity
dependency was an explicit earlier ordering decision and needs a disposition, not an
