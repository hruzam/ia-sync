## Why this session exists

The bed is built; the mechanism that governs it is not. `~/reposoma/raw.incarnations/`
(prepared, pending majkee gavel, reposoma 7a58e92) holds per-seat loading spines — the

## The open design questions (the meat — enumerated, NOT answered here)

1. **Birth.** When is a seat mature enough to seed? What event mints the first seed —
   an arc close, an operator call, a scar threshold?
2. **Authorship — the load-bearing one.** Self-authored (the seat models itself) risks the
   introspection blind spot: a seat cannot see its own performance (G-42), so a self-seed
   may inherit flattery as scar. Does a seed require a CROSSED WITNESS — another seat that
   *worked with* this one attesting the scars are real? This is the counter-sign principle
   (lived this session: Cartan NOK→NOK→OK on the wrapper) applied to inheritance itself.
3. **Cross-vendor acceptance — codex vs claude.** One seed schema both vendors read? Can a
   claude seat's seed be witnessed/accepted by a codex seat and vice versa? Is a cross-vendor
   witness REQUIRED by construction, so the mechanism is decorrelated (G-26/G-31) rather than
   claude-sole? @Cartan seats the codex side so the answer is not translated after the fact.
4. **Maintenance.** Behavioral-change-per-token as the admission test; append-only spine;
   who prunes a scar to history when it stops bending a hand; how bloat is prevented (G-16).
5. **Graduation / the recall→reflex frontier.** Does the mechanism stop at retrieval
   (prompt-layer = fast recall, the honest-limit clause: KNOW not FEEL, G-15) or reach toward
   the weight layer (fine-tune on the gavel bed = true reflex)? Scope call, likely phased.
6. **Load.** Selective, trigger-indexed inheritance — only the task-relevant scars enter
   context, keyed by the cold-start card. Context spent scales with relevance, not history.
7. **Bed relationships.** How the seed DRAWS FROM therapy + gavels without forking their
   authority (point-never-copy is already the raw.incarnations law).
