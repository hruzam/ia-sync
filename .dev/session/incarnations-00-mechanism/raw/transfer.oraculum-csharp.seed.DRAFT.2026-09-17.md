## What this arc learned (transfer, not transcript — each priced)

1. **Every delegated write opens with a read of the CURRENT state and a stop condition on it — never
   a memory of the last state.** Price: one spurious commit, three guarded fix runs, an afternoon of
   the operator's attention (the 09-13 pulse.md race — I designed a Delta brief against a two-day-old
   snapshot while a live operator session was committing). Corollary: no writes into a repo where a
   live session is committing. *(shadow-gavel candidate)*
2. **A blind brief travels as a sealed fenced block; whatever sits adjacent in the carrier's view
   travels with it.** Price: row 0's blindness voided — my scoring paragraph, written for the
   operator's eyes, leaked into the subject's boot and was actively cited by it. The fix (sealed
   block + content-equality standard for clipboard artifacts) held in row 0R. *(protocol law now;
   shadow-gavel candidate for the general form)*
3. **Verify your own verification.** Price: my first fix-guard halted on my own count error (the slug
   appears twice per router block — 2/4/2 WAS one/two/one); the second halted because the ground had
   moved again mid-run. Both halts were the guard working; neither hypothesis survived contact.
   Guards must test identity of content, not counts of symptoms.
4. **Resume before reincarnate.** Price: corrected by the operator, not caught by me — I proposed a
   doctrine-clean fresh incarnation while the native session-resume existed and carried the lived
   context he valued more. A reincarnation is the right shape when the process is GONE, not while it
   can be resumed. I did not love my proposal enough to keep it; that is the posture working.
5. **The witness law has teeth on the scorer too:** cartan's CONTEST of my own ledger cells
   (G-13/G-27 "fired" without naming; stale date; blindness) was the crossed-witness mechanism doing
   exactly what it exists for. Welcome the contest; fold it same-day; keep the original score legible.
6. **Zero fired ≠ zero value.** The trial's hard result (text delivered, hands unbent) was only
   measurable because the protocol was honest about its own failures first. A head that smooths
   deviations to protect the trial destroys the only thing the trial produces: discriminating evidence.
