**attest** means the cited evidence supports this exact line at the witness's declared
scope. For a birth witness it includes the price paid and the gavel's wire back to the
arc; for a claim witness it supports the measured observation. Neither is a gavel.

**contest** means a cited observation contradicts the claim, or the score applies a
different rule from the pre-registration. State the narrower supported claim or the
correction to test. A documented contradiction is not can't-see.

**can't-see** means the evidence or vantage is insufficient. Name the exact missing
path/receipt needed to decide. For a birth line, L2 leaves that line in therapy rather
than admitting it through this attestation. R2 separately permits an explicitly
self-composed, operator-gaveled seed with witness: pending; this form does not silently
convert pending or can't-see into attest.

A gavel ID establishes a locked entry; it does not by itself prove the witness shared
the price. A seed read establishes access; it does not prove effect. A claimed reason
plus compatible action meets the trial's operational fired rule; causal attribution
still needs its own evidence. Reaching a trigger and misclassifying it is not evidence
that the task never reached it.

Before returning a load verdict, inspect the **actual** first task message and read
results: session/time/CWD, any scoring text delivered to the subject, seed identity,
selected versus returned gavel ranges, read-before-edit order, and the action receipts.
Use immutable commits after a worktree changes; distinguish the boot outcome from a
later human approval or revert. Keep runtime histories host-local and cite record
numbers. Promote necessary mail evidence to the session bus before citing it.

owner of each next action. The head folds claim scores into its ledger/STATUS; majkee
