   hand relay between the same head and BUS when the tunnel fails. Declare both at

   its reply, reconcile available evidence before asking Majkee to relay the same
   work. Avoid duplicate assignments and overlapping turns on the same head.
5. **Operator enable and bounded cost.** Use only the table and work the operator

6. **Recoverable continuation.** On wake/resume, resolve the bed, actual target,
   current STATUS, outstanding receipt, and local transport handle. Retain the same
   head when relaying; a deliberate head replacement needs an explicit handoff.
7. **Temporary lifetime.** Name the owning bed, activation, retirement, and cleanup
