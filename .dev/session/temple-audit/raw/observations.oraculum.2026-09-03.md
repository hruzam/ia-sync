# Observations for the temple audit

## 1 · The method should be selection, not review

Canon that survived these three days did so by governing real decisions under pressure —
not by being read. Audit each existing doctrine/canon claim with three questions:

- **Receipt test:** did it govern an actual decision in the last ~60 days? (cite or kill)
- **Dead-letter test:** was it violated without harm? (then it was never law — bury it)
- **One-source test:** does it duplicate a claim that lives elsewhere? (fold, point, delete)

Killed doctrine goes to the **legacy wall**, not to /dev/null — the culture layer keeps
what the disk-contract drops. The /octo retirement gave the audit its best criterion:
*"a card that describes what everyone already does earns nothing."* Its inverse is also
true: a law nobody yet follows is an aspiration, and aspirations are drafts, not canon.

## 2 · What actually governed well (codify THESE — they have receipts)

- **Receipts/gavels/dated verdicts over determinism** (Sella L8) — governed everything:
  the tunnel FAIL→fix→PASS, the Mirror REVISE on HANDSHAKE, the Cartan preflight catch.
- **Behavior proof before promotion** — the shim stayed undeployed until t3 passed; the
  discipline caught a real defect fixtures missed. Strongest law in the house.
- **Point-never-copy + one source per claim** — the housecleaning's whole engine.
- **Delivery rule** (silence ≠ receipt; consumption stamped) — born from a cross-vendor

  collisions. The convergence held BECAUSE of these two habits.

- **The FAIL is part of the proof** — t06 evidence keeps both attempts. Epistemically the

## 5 · Structural lean (argue with me)

Three layers, one visible hierarchy, so a fresh agent needs zero folklore:

```
global law            — ONE short file (lean: reposoma/raw.canon/), few stones, each
                        with receipts; everything below cites it upward
canon stones          — raw.canon/: per-domain (project-topology, session-shape,
                        primitive-building=Sella-core, cross-vendor=HANDSHAKE)
guides                — raw.guides/: HOW-to only; zero authority claims; point up
session surfaces      — beds, buses, journals: tense-separated, die with their gates
temple/               — culture: walls, roster, decisions-history; no machine reads it
```

Note Sella and HANDSHAKE become canon *stones* under global law — their content barely
changes; only their WINS clauses learn to bow.

## 6 · One warning

The audit must not become the new bureaucracy. One session, one gate — something like:
*"every supremacy claim seated under the global-law file + every dead letter buried on
the wall."* Timeboxed. If the audit spawns a framework, it has failed by its own §3.
