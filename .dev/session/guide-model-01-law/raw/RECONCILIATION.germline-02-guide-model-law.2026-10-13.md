### Accepted planning directions — 2026-10-14

1. Every binding operational instruction belongs in a public germline program.
2. GUIDE is the book and may carry discretionary judgment, not a second maintained recipe.
3. A program has controller, bounded function, and owner-held model/state roles; these names
   remain working terms pending the naming gavel.
4. A public program serves any seat, vendor, chatbot, or human with the needed capability;
   it is not an agent card.
5. Program grain is larger than a skill and smaller than a whole guide.
6. Vendor skills/agents are thin loaders, never alternate procedure homes.
7. Cards are private controllers that hold grants/the pen; programs are public controllers that
   require capabilities and never grant them.
8. A private card may point to a public program, never the reverse; promotion leaves one home
   for a method and a pointer from the former private card.
9. Program selection is explicit and bounded: the controller's functions table is the only
   function index; a loader resolves a selected program/function and does not discover work.

### Required reader contract

An agent whose task matches a program reads its controller plus selected function and explicitly
named task/model inputs. GUIDE must not become an undeclared prerequisite for correct execution.
A GUIDE may discuss the judgment around an operation, but it may not keep a second maintained
strict recipe for it.
