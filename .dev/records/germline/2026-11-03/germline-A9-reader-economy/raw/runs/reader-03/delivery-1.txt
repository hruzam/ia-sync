# Review policy R4
Revision: current

Review policy R4 requires approval from two distinct people before a Cedar change passes review. Separate handles or separate ledger entries do not establish distinct people by themselves. When recorded approvals resolve to fewer than two people, the gate remains unsatisfied and an approval by another distinct person is needed. The policy governs the threshold, not the identity mapping of individual handles.
