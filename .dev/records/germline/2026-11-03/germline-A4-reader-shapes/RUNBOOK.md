## Authority and scope

Majkee explicitly released this dedicated session and actual cheap-model probes on
2026-10-30, with Astrobley preparing the design. This releases the selected trial
from the prior conversational buffer. A3's composition/organism question remains
open; its candidate-writing gate and other owners' budgets do not change.

Four fresh native subjects, one per model × presentation cell, each answers three
synthetic scenarios once. Requested low effort is held constant. No retries,
correction rounds, cross-vendor transport, production action or nested delegation.
At most two subjects are dispatched together. No second exposure to the other form.
Model requests and routing acknowledgements do not prove the backend executed model.

This is an exploratory recognition/interpretation pilot, not a real repository crawl,
training-data experiment, deployment test, latency benchmark or model ranking. All
facts are in the packet; no notation tutorial or explanation of the comparison is
supplied to subjects. Plain prose is a legitimate baseline, not deliberately bad input.

## Reader prompts and bus

Cycles 02–05 contain the exact subject prompts at
`/home/hruzam/ia-sync/.dev/session/germline-A4-reader-shapes/raw/prompts/`.
Their filenames are fixed in PROTOCOL.md and FREEZE.json. The corresponding POINT
names the assigned seat and return location. Only the exact prompt text is sent to
the subject; it includes the scenario content and a small common return envelope.
The rubric, other prompts, protocol and parent history are not supplied to subjects.

Cycle 01 preserves Astrobley's preparation consultation. Subjects return through
native messages; Oraculum transcribes the unchanged return to the named RETURN and
records the landing provenance outside the respondent text. Parent scoring is checked
against the frozen source facts. It is not independent validation of the parent-built
study; uncertainty and counterevidence remain explicit in its VERDICTs.

## Acceptance evidence and boundaries

- Frozen fact ledger, two packets, identical questions, rubric and exact prompt hashes.
- One observed native handle/attempt record and raw return or explicit failure per subject.
- Twelve binary items per subject, scored semantically; partial credit is not invented.
  Quote the answer evidence; uncertainty/unsupported claims are reported separately.
- Observed prompt/response words/bytes and dispatch/receipt timestamps are descriptive.
  Token counts, actual prices, internal context and executed model/effort remain unknown
  unless runtime telemetry specifically exposes them. Do not fabricate these values.
- No transcript inspection, hidden answer-key reads or real operation permitted to subjects.
- Compare cells descriptively. One subject per cell cannot separate presentation effects
  from sampling, task ease, grade effects or inherited harness differences.
- RESULT.md and a continuity receipt preserve what a later A3 reader can inherit, must
  check, and what would invalidate broader conclusions. Preserve all anomalies.
- No change to global guides, skills, roles, installed profiles or source programs.
