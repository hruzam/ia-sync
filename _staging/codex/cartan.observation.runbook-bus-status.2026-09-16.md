## 1. RUNBOOK — select the work before selecting the ceremony

**Observed:** the gate's “reference/label-only” wording understated schema/authority
seams that were explicitly gaveled. Later runtime, peer-presence and proof work made
the arc wider than its opening shorthand. The accepted scope was not unauthorized;
the stale description still made orientation harder. My own launcher also retained
invalid YAML and three dead protocol references while I was judging other producers.
Those four launcher defects were repaired at closeout.

**My conclusion:** make the observable outcome and instrument choice clear up front.
Full independent sessions earn their cost for consequential design, ownership and
crossed review; ordinary patches need not automatically inherit the heaviest ceremony.
That is a recommendation for choosing the next task's shape, not permission to replace
an already-declared independent session with a child agent.

Validate the actual launcher before activation. Keep its references exact. When the
gate really changes, follow the existing sibling-session law rather than silently
rewriting history. Offer a bounded STOP/re-scope choice sooner when probes stop adding
useful evidence. No arbitrary pass-rate quota or universal cycle limit was established.

**Fold disposition:** mostly application of existing [RUNBOOK law](/home/hruzam/reposoma/raw.guides/runbook/GUIDE.md)
— “The rule that delimits a session,” “Structure,” and “Style laws.” A small tested
launcher example could help more than another paragraph of injunctions; that is an
optional future candidate, not an authored template here.

## 5. Operator, Git and concurrent work — carriage should be lighter

**Observed:** fresh remote checks and disjoint-scope checks prevented stale Git actions.
Parallel work also changed deployed cards before every arc owner knew. Majkee confirmed
the therapy-associated deploy was authorized: that is a visibility/coordination lesson,
not evidence of an unauthorized deployment. Peer Trajectory was a sovereign cSharp,
not my delegate. Its board-client contract was coordinated before client binding.

I repeatedly announced commit boundaries instead of explaining their purpose once.
Majkee reasonably expected reversible local checkpoints. Diffs/hashes compare bytes;
commits preserve work; joins update coordination state; push/deploy cross other boundaries.
They are not interchangeable gates and need not occur in the same commit.

**My conclusion:** agree scoped Git authority once, then act within it. Ask again when
scope, risk or external state materially changes, not to repeat an already-set boundary.
Protect another writer's staged/dirty work. Give the operator one exact next relay and
identify any already-authorized independent work that can proceed beside it.
Carry shared-deploy targets and notice through the operator; presence never supplies permission.

The local transcript-pickup option was approved for either runtime pairing subject to
access. That is not proof of all four pairings, universal transport law, or permission
to browse unrelated histories. For a future connector, manually confirmed endpoint
bindings and an exact payload preview come before delivery automation. Availability,
consumption, verification and STATUS join remain different facts. Ambiguous submission
needs reconciliation, not blind retry. The earlier Nablarva advisory handoff stays
project-owned; this note neither reassigns nor certifies that project's current work.

## 7. Majkee's addition — inexpensive continuity, expensive judgment on demand

**Operator observation, September 16:** keeping an expensive model as the whole-arc
cSharp seemed to burn too much for too little. Prefer strong heads for a well-prepared
RUNBOOK, a cheaper coordinator between capable executives/architects, and checks that
no question or obligation has been forgotten. Sonnet/Luna or a lighter seat were
offered as examples, with capability checks; living notifications were raised as a
possible runbook-tool improvement. This is the operator's purpose-fit assessment,
not a measured model-price comparison or a gavel changing any role contract.

**My proposed refinement:** keep one accountable head, but separate continuity work
from exceptional judgment. Do not pay for an LLM to poll filenames or rehash unchanged
evidence. Let deterministic tooling carry observable facts; let a qualified economical
controller maintain the story within the agreed plan; call the stronger architect or
independent verifier when a decision actually needs it.

| Responsibility | Proposed instrument | Escalation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Check file shape, coordinates, referenced paths and declared outstanding branches | Read-only deterministic checker in the runbook tool | Malformed, ambiguous, unsupported or conflicting input remains visible; never repaired by guessing |
| Maintain the bounded story and perform routine authorized coordination | Capability-tested economical head; still the sole declared STATUS owner | Changed gate/scope, contradictory evidence, uncertain execution or an unfamiliar decision goes to the architect/operator |
| Design the plan, resolve difficult semantic questions, verify consequential claims | Strong architect or named independent reviewer, called for a bounded decision | No permanent re-reading of the whole history merely to acknowledge progress |
| Gavel, authorize changed boundaries and choose costly tradeoffs | Majkee | The checker and cheap coordinator cannot manufacture approval |

This is a candidate for the next comparable arc, not a swap of today's stopped head
or a new global agent. Model family alone does not qualify the coordinator. Reuse the
arc's discriminating cases: stale STATUS plus a landed terminal VERDICT; REVISE linked
to a corrective cycle; wrong seat/return path; unknown CLI liveness; conflicting work;
an informational peer POINT that owes no RETURN. A coordinator should either give the
safe next action or escalate, without inventing success or redispatching uncertain work.
A “no forgotten work” check covers **declared** obligations, not every possible implicit
question in natural-language history. Important new questions must be surfaced explicitly.

### What the runbook tool already has, and the smallest useful addition

Source inspection on September 16, not a live TUI test:
[runbook.py](/home/hruzam/ia-sync/zsh/session/runbook.py) already has newest-first cycle
grouping and a literal “return file present · no verdict file” hint (`render_bus_group`).
Its UI has a one-second tick; the selected bed refreshes from STATUS mtime or a changed
BUS filename set, and the presence board has its own refresh key. `bus_key` contains
filenames, not receipt-content fingerprints. These are existing foundations, not missing
features to rebuild or evidence of a complete semantic obligation checker.

**First addition I would ask Trajectory to assess: one live, operator-facing attention
view across explicitly selected beds.** Compute it from RUNBOOK/STATUS/BUS, without a
new shared inbox, scheduler or fourth BUS kind. Useful rows would name the fact and link
the exact artifact:

- New or changed RETURN observed; content/binding checks passed or a specific parse
  limit is shown. This is “review may be needed,” not “work succeeded.”
- STATUS still awaits a verdict for which a correctly bound terminal VERDICT exists:
  “owner reconciliation needed,” not an automatic join.
- A declared obligation has a missing artifact, wrong seat/cycle/path, unresolved
  corrective link or contradictory state. Missing is not “failed,” and elapsed time
  is not proof a worker died.
- An explicit operator decision remains outstanding. Derive this only from supported
  declared fields/content; free-text ambiguity stays unclassified and linked for review.

“Living notification” should first mean a changing badge or optional operator alert
with bed, cycle, reason and open/copy action. It must not wake a seat, send a prompt,
rewrite STATUS or claim receipt. Detect change without treating initial file creation
as completed publication: a partial file may later finish without a filename change.
Content stability or successful parsing alone still does not attest the writer or result.
Recompute after restart; suppress repeated unchanged alerts as local UI state, not BUS
read-state. Start with the running TUI; a background service is not yet justified.

This gives the operator useful attention signals without giving an expensive model a
continuous polling job. A later one-action relay can reuse the earlier endpoint/preview/
ambiguity rules, under separately authorized transport. Presence stays advisory and
cannot supply a session endpoint or a wake permission.

The prospective comparison should measure operator interventions, context reprocessing,
time to a correct next action, missed obligations, false alarms and unsafe duplicate
dispatches on the same bounded task class. Success is lower coordination burden with
preserved judgment, not merely fewer tokens or a cheaper model name. No such comparison
was run here. The old telemetry measures repeated processing, not an idle-window charge
or a price attribution to the head role.
