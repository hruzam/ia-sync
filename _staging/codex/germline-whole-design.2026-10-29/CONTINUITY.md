- Revision identity, actual body delivery, understanding and observed behavior are
  different claims. A preview can expose a stamp without delivering the full body.
  The README's stronger stamp wording is a recorded disagreement, not a resolved fix.
- Forge contains an explicit stale-topology warning. The old render/symlink picture
  cannot be treated as the current deployment design.
- “Function from anywhere” means a resolved permitted source and known dependencies.
  A relationship record or reference does not grant effects or determine call order.
- G09 functional behavior did not establish general germline contribution or reduced
  head burden. Keep PRODUCT, DELEGATION and GERMLINE dispositions distinct.
- G07's missing-result report conflicted with durable completed receipts. Inspect
  receipts before considering retries; do not manufacture another tunnel operation.
- A cache may be rebuildable; an original observation or decision needs durable custody.
  Vectors may aid relevance but cannot by themselves decide currentness or authority.
- C2C/AKV were researched through primary sources, not installed or tested here.
  Their model-side cache operations do not establish access to this CLI's internal KV.
- Documentation role separation can be borrowed from Nablarva. Its locks and stale
