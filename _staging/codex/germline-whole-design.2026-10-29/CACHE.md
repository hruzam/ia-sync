## Analytical inheritance — hypotheses, not locks

| Interpretation | Evidence and limit | What would change the view |
|---|---|---|
| Familiar forms can be sufficient when their meaning is clear. | A4/A5 bounded successes; no demonstrated format winner or training-data mechanism. | Comparable tasks where familiar labels mislead, or a different representation materially improves justified decisions. |
| Less delivery can still expose more repeated history. | A9 adjudicated identity pair: 8/8 versus 8/8, 48.8% lower stored input, 23.9% higher visible-history proxy. Outside-read effects, native carriage and billing remain unverified. | Reliable end-to-end telemetry or a controlled access/helper comparison; do not pool the three different experiments into one accuracy rate. |
| Continuity is valuable when a receiver recovers the right understanding at the point of need. | Staged depth and receipts are candidates; document accumulation can itself add reading burden. | Failed fresh uptake, excessive recovery cost, or receipt maintenance costing more than it saves. |
| Exploration needs anomaly coverage within bounded authority. | Directed mapping and frontier exploration are complementary proposals; product success alone does not establish architectural contribution. | Persistent false leads, costly correction, missed anomalies or a smaller method achieving the same useful coverage. |

This CACHE is local, uncommitted research at creation. Later teams should inherit its

their sources change. Promotion to a guide, skill or canon remains separately owned.
