# Reconcile contract

Draft example, not the live G09 recovery instructions.

## Full instructions

Inspect the assigned receipt and its exact evidence. Separate observation from inference.
Resolve permitted recovery from the governing contract. Return the known identity, remaining
uncertainty and one bounded next action. An uncertain submission is not evidence for replay.
