## A9: less delivery can still mean more conversation exposure

Scores below incorporate [Cartan's adjudication](../../../.dev/session/germline-A9-reader-economy/raw/SCORING-ADDENDUM.md).

`analyze.py` still reproduces the original scores and flags, not this addendum.

| Case | Full / selective score | Delivered-input reduction | Visible-history exposure change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Readiness | 7/8 · 0/8 | 49.2% less | 18.8% more |
| Review identity | 8/8 · 8/8 | 48.8% less | 23.9% more |
| Interrupted operation | 8/8 · 0/8 | 73.0% less | 73.0% less, but no documents retrieved and no supported decision |

Delivered input (D) counts stored task/index/protocol/document payload bytes.
Exposure (E) sums the visible input and preceding output available at each response,
assuming retained history. E illustrates the cost of repeated context; it is not
backend usage or billing. Neither caches, hidden reasoning nor harness context are
measured here. Real token and monetary cost remain unknown.

The 0/8 readiness answer included some correct directions but supplied no citations
and had not read the inventory. Its zero is an evidence-scoring result, not a claim
that every sentence was false. The recovery reader replied `ANSWER` while asking for
documents, ending its attempt under the fixed protocol. That demonstrates an interface
failure in this run, not inability to reason from recovery evidence it never received.
